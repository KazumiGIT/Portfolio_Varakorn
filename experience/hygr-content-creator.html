Content Creator at HYGR

261+ videos. 38 million views. Every state in Malaysia. The chapter where I learned how attention works — by earning it the slow way.

RoleContent Creator
PeriodJan 2024 – Aug 2025
Views38,000,000+
ReachAll 13 states of Malaysia

The job

HYGR is a Malaysian personal care brand known for its natural deodorant and tinted lip balm. I joined as their content creator in January 2024: script, shoot, edit, post, read the numbers, repeat. Two product lines, every platform, no crew. By the time I left in August 2025 the counter said 261+ videos and 38 million views.

Learning the algorithm by feeding it

You cannot read your way into understanding distribution. The only teacher is volume with attention: ship, watch where people leave, change one thing, ship again. After a few hundred cycles the algorithm stops being mysterious — it's just brutally honest audience research, at speed.

  • Hooks rewritten five, six, ten times — the first two seconds are the whole video.
  • A tracker for which hook variant went where; performance logs that taught the next round.
  • The unglamorous automation behind the scenes that kept daily shipping possible — my first taste of the work I now do professionally.

Thirteen states, four languages

HYGR also put me on booth sales: malls, night markets, and expos in all 13 states of Malaysia, pitching in English, Malay, Chinese, or Thai depending on who walked up. Watching a stranger's face while you pitch teaches you things a retention graph never will.

Why this chapter still matters

People assume I left content behind when I became an engineer. The opposite: every AI marketing workflow I build now — at P10X Media and Orion Automation — ends at a human deciding whether to keep watching. I just automate everything before that moment.
