Why I registered a studio instead of taking a job

After graduating from Gamuda AI Academy, the sensible move was a junior engineering role. Instead I registered Orion Automation with SSM, because the thing I'm actually good at is the combination: build the agent, write the landing page, film the explainer, close the client — in English, Malay, Chinese, or Thai.

What the studio does

  • AI agents & RAG — tool-calling agents and retrieval pipelines that ship to production, not demos.
  • Business process automation — the quiet systems that post, parse, track, and reply while the owner sleeps. FastAPI backends, n8n flows.
  • Chatbots — WhatsApp and web bots that answer the same eight questions so a human doesn't have to.
  • SEO · AEO · GEO — search built for humans, answer engines, and generative engines. Being the source that gets cited, not just ranked.

Who it's for

The businesses that need automation most are the ones least likely to buy "AI transformation": a kedai owner copying WhatsApp orders into a spreadsheet at 11pm, a clinic answering the same appointment questions every day.

Orion works online-first from its base in Subang Jaya, serving businesses across Selangor and Kuala Lumpur — USJ, Sunway, Petaling Jaya, Damansara, Shah Alam, Klang, Puchong, Seri Kembangan, Kajang, Bangi, Ampang, Cheras, Gombak, Selayang, Rawang, Sepang, Cyberjaya, and Putrajaya — and remotely with clients anywhere in Malaysia.

Every engagement starts with a busywork audit: find the tasks that are repeated, rule-based, and resented — then make them go away. Unglamorous, measurable, easy to say yes to.

Where it's heading

These days the studio runs alongside my role as AI Specialist at P10X Media — same craft, two arenas. The systems I build by day sharpen the ones I ship at night.

FAQ

Quick answers

What is Orion Automation?

An SSM-registered AI automation studio I founded in Subang Jaya, Selangor. It builds AI agents, business process automation, chatbots, custom web solutions, and handles SEO, AEO and GEO for businesses that want their busywork to disappear.

What services does Orion Automation offer?

AI agents and RAG pipelines, business process automation (BPA), WhatsApp and web chatbots, workflow automation with FastAPI and n8n, custom websites, and search visibility across SEO, AEO (answer engines), and GEO (generative engines like ChatGPT and Gemini).

Where does Orion Automation operate?

Online-first from Subang Jaya, serving businesses across Selangor and Kuala Lumpur — USJ, Sunway, Petaling Jaya, Damansara, Shah Alam, Klang, Puchong, Seri Kembangan, Kajang, Bangi, Ampang, Cheras, Gombak, Selayang, Rawang, Sepang, Cyberjaya, and Putrajaya — plus remote work with clients anywhere in Malaysia.

Is Orion Automation a registered business in Malaysia?

Yes — registered with SSM (Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia), founded in 2026 after I graduated from Gamuda AI Academy.

What kind of businesses benefit from automation?

Any business with tasks that are repeated, rule-based, and resented: orders copied from WhatsApp into spreadsheets, the same eight customer questions answered daily, invoices chased by hand. Six hours a week saved is 300+ hours a year returned to the business.
