College Community Pasir Salak

The origin chapter: a Sijil Teknologi Maklumat in Pasir Salak, Perak, where I discovered I liked building things more than studying them.

CredentialCert in Information Technology
PeriodAug 2022 to Jan 2024
LocationPasir Salak, Perak, Malaysia
ClassSijil Teknologi Maklumat

The program

Kolej Komuniti Pasir Salak is a Malaysian community college in Perak. If you're picturing a glass campus with a startup incubator, adjust downward considerably. What it did have was a syllabus wide enough to touch everything:

  • Web development: HTML, CSS, and PHP; the first time something I typed showed up on a screen and did what I said.
  • Game development: Unity and Unreal Engine projects.
  • Mobile apps: a toolchain from idea to installable.
  • IoT experiments: wiring sensors to boards and arguing about why nothing lit up.

Breadth beat depth, and that was lucky

Nothing went deep, and in hindsight that was the point: four chances in two years to find the thing that makes you lose track of time. I was a mediocre student in the parts that required memorising and a much better one in the parts that required building. That gap told me something I didn't act on for another three years.

The habit that survived

The specific technologies aged out. PHP is not in my stack, and I haven't opened Unreal since. What survived is the reflex doing all the work in my career right now: when I don't understand something, I build a small version of it. That reflex carried me from a computer lab in Perak through the HYGR camera years, a Python bootcamp, and Gamuda AI Academy, to building AI systems professionally in Selangor today.

FAQ

Quick answers

What did Varakorn study at College Community Pasir Salak?

The Sijil Teknologi Maklumat, the Certificate in Information Technology, from August 2022 to January 2024: web development with HTML, CSS and PHP, game development in Unity and Unreal Engine, mobile apps, and IoT experiments.

What is a Sijil Teknologi Maklumat?

Malay for Certificate in Information Technology: a practical, hands on IT qualification offered by Malaysia's community college (Kolej Komuniti) system.

Where is College Community Pasir Salak?

Kolej Komuniti Pasir Salak is in the Pasir Salak district of Perak, Malaysia.

What stuck from the program?

The reflex that still drives my career: when I don't understand something, I build a small version of it. The technologies aged out; the builder's habit survived.

How did an IT certificate lead to AI engineering?

The certificate gave me breadth, web, games, mobile, IoT, and showed me I liked building more than studying. After the HYGR creator chapter, a Python bootcamp in December 2025, and Gamuda AI Academy in 2026, that foundation became a full stack AI engineering career.
