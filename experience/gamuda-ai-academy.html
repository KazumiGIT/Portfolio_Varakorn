Gamuda AI Academy

Three months of full stack AI engineering at the KL Campus — and the capstone that ended with a demo to Malaysia's Minister of Science, Technology and Innovation. The leap.

RoleTrainee · Team Lead
PeriodJan – Mar 2026 · Cohort 5
LocationKuala Lumpur, Malaysia

The program

Gamuda AI Academy is an intensive AI engineering program in Malaysia — an initiative by Yayasan Gamuda, supported by Gamuda and Google Cloud. I was selected into Cohort 5 at the KL Campus (January to March 2026) after pivoting from content creation through a Python bootcamp.

The syllabus was the full pipeline from data to interface: Python, FastAPI, React, Langchain, RAG and vector retrieval, agentic workflows, and system integration. Daily, intensively, for three months.

Tendervise AI — the capstone I led

As project team lead, I ran the build of Tendervise AI: an agent that reads construction tender documents (hundreds of pages of requirements and compliance language), extracts the requirements, and scores each tender against ESG criteria — targeting a 50% cut in tender response time.

  • FastAPI backend, RAG over the tender corpus, Langchain orchestrating agent steps, Postgres holding structured results.
  • Explainable scoring, so a human reviewer can trust and verify every number.
  • Team of four, eight weeks, one demo that had to land.

Three minutes with a minister

We presented the prototype to YB Chang Lih Kang, Malaysia's Minister of Science, Technology and Innovation. Eight weeks of engineering compressed into three minutes: tenders take days, this takes hours, here's the proof. That March I also gave the graduation speech for the cohort — both moments were the same skill, and it wasn't one I learned at the academy. It came from two years on camera and sales floors.

What it unlocked

A month after graduating I registered Orion Automation in Subang Jaya; two months after that, P10X Media hired me as their AI Specialist. The academy turned a creator who could code a little into an engineer who ships.
