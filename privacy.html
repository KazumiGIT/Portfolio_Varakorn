The paperwork

Privacy Policy

What this site knows about you, where it lives, and how to make it forget.

Last updated 31 August 2026

The plain version

  • Browse without signing in and this site stores nothing about you.
  • Sign in with Google and it keeps your name, email and profile photo, so your comments have a face.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or tracked. There are no analytics and no advertising cookies here.
  • Email varakornm0403@gmail.com and anything stored about you is deleted.

Who runs this site

This is the personal portfolio of Varakorn (legal name Varakorn Meunukdomn), based in Subang Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia. For anything in this policy, write to varakornm0403@gmail.com.

What is collected, and when

When youThe site keepsWhy
Just browse Nothing personal. Your theme choice (day or night) stays in your own browser and is never sent anywhere. So the lamp stays the way you left it.
Talk to the desk terminal Nothing. Your messages are sent to Google Gemini to generate the reply, then forgotten. Treat it like a postcard and do not type anything private. The chatbot needs a brain, and the brain is Google Gemini.
Sign in with Google Your name, email address, profile photo, and the account identifier Google provides. So your comments and likes belong to you and show your name.
Fill in your profile Whatever you choose to add: a title, a short bio, links to your socials. These are public and show on your profile card in the guestbook. So your comments and vouches can say who you are.
Comment or like The comment text, the page it was left on, the time, and a scrambled version of your network address. The real address is not stored. To show the guestbook, and to stop one person flooding it.

As account features grow (saved posts, reading progress and similar), the site stores what you deliberately save, tied to your account, and this page is updated to say so.

Where it lives

Account and guestbook data is stored with Supabase in Singapore. The site itself is hosted by Vercel, which keeps ordinary server logs the way nearly every host does.

The services this site relies on

  • Google sign in confirms who you are. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google does.
  • Google Gemini generates the desk terminal's replies from what you type.
  • Supabase stores accounts and comments, in Singapore.
  • Vercel serves the site.

That is the whole list. No analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no social media trackers.

Cookies

One cookie, and only after you sign in: it keeps you signed in. Signing out removes it. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act, and as plain courtesy, you can ask at any time to see what is stored about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email varakornm0403@gmail.com from the address on your account and it is handled, usually within a few days. You can also delete your own comments from your account page once signed in.

How long things are kept

Comments stay until you delete them or ask for them to be removed. Accounts stay until you ask for deletion. Deleting an account removes its comments and likes with it.

Children

This site is not aimed at children, and accounts are meant for people old enough to hold a Google account under Google's own rules.

When this policy changes

The date at the top moves, and meaningful changes get a plain note here. No silent edits.
